Learning model

Learn.
Build.
Demonstrate.

ALFA Academy is designed around practical learning rather than knowledge that exists only inside a textbook.

Learners should be able to understand a concept, apply it, demonstrate it and document what they have built.

The long-term goal is to create a learning environment where skills translate into real capability.
